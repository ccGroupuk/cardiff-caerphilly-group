While you wait

Make it safe first.

Only do what you can do safely. If in doubt, leave it and call us.

Water leak or burst pipe

Turn the water off at the stopcock (usually under the kitchen sink). Switch off the boiler or immersion heater, then run the cold taps to drain the pipes.

Electrics

If it is safe to reach, switch off the main switch on your consumer unit (fuse box). Never touch anything that is wet, sparking or hot.

Water near electrics

Keep away from wet sockets and light fittings. Turn the power off at the consumer unit if you can reach it dry, then the water off at the stopcock.

Smell gas? Do not use light switches or flames, open the windows, and call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999 straight away. It is free and open 24 hours.

Is the emergency line really 24 hours?

Yes. Call 029 2169 0437 any time, day or night, for burst pipes, leaks and other plumbing emergencies, or send an emergency WhatsApp to 07545 514746.

What counts as an electrical emergency?

Losing all power, a burning smell, a sparking socket, or a breaker that will not stay on. Call our electricians on 029 2169 0502. More on emergency electrics.

Water is coming through a light fitting. Who do I call?

Both, which is why we are one team. Switch the electrics off at the consumer unit if you can do it safely and keep away from the wet fitting, turn the water off at the stopcock, then call 029 2169 0437. Our plumbers stop the leak and our electricians check the circuit before it goes back on.
